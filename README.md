# ms274_project

These are my files for my MS 274 project.

Project: Examining how ENSO events affect distribution of phytoplankton productivity differently 
for Atlantic regions of coastal upwelling and Pacific regions of coastal upwelling. 

Abstract: The affect of ENSO events on eastern Pacific upwelling systems are well documented; El Nino
events drastically reduce upwelling through weakening trade winds, while La Nina events increase
upwelling through the strengthening of the trade winds. Upwelling is a vital process to the success
of coastal marine ecosystems, bringing up cold, nutrient-dense water that feeds primary-producing 
phytoplankton, the foundation of coastal marine food chains. Because of this relationship, phytoplankton 
productivity (using chlorophyll a) is a good proxy for effect of ENSO phase on coastal ecosystem health. 
I want to examine how major ENSO events affect zones of coastal upwelling differently in the Atlantic 
and Pacific via chlorophyll a concentration. I want to examine the temporal difference in effect of ENSO 
event on phytoplankton productivity for these two regions to better understand how each ENSO phase 
affects the marine food chain differently.

Outline: To analyze the differences in coastal upwelling for the Atlantic and the Pacific oceans I have 
chosen two study areas: the Humboldt Current system for the eastern Pacific, and the Canary Current 
System in the eastern Atlantic. For downloading data, coordinates for these two locations will be 
latitude (-20 to -10) and longitude (-80 to -70) for the Humboldt Current System and latitude (20 to 30)
and longitude (-20 to -10) for the Canary Current System. To compare these two systems, I will create
a time series of chlorophyll a concentration, SST, and alongshore wind data for each system from 2003
to 2026. For vector wind data, I will use the L4 Cross-Calibrated Multi-Platform (CCMP) product from 
NASA Earthaccess (the same dataset as used in Lab 8). For SST data I will use the Multi-scale Ultra-high 
Resolution (MUR) Sea Surface Temperature (SST) product supported by the NASA MEaSUREs program 
(the same data set as used in Lab 5). For chlorophyll a data I will be using the Ocean Colour component 
of the European Space Agency Climate Change Initiative project (ESA OC-CCI). To represent the results 
of this analysis, I plan on creating a time series of each variable (wind, SST, and chlorophyll a 
concentration) for both locations. I plan to calculate anomalies for SST, alongshore wind, and 
chlorophyll a concentrations for the desired time frame (2003-2026). I also plan on making a Hovmoller 
diagram for the anomalies of each variable, with the y axis as time and and the x axis as latitude or
longitude. It may be helpful to generate a version of each with both latitude and longitude as the
x axis to identify all notable differences.
