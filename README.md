# ms274_project

These are my files for my MS 274 project.

Project: Examining how ENSO events affect distribution of phytoplankton productivity differently 
for Atlantic regions of coastal upwelling and Pacific regions of coastal upwelling. 

Abstract: The affect of ENSO events on eastern Pacific upwelling systems are well documented; El Nino
events drastically reduce upwelling through weakening trade winds, while La Nina events increase
upwelling through the strengthening of the trade winds. Upwelling is a vital process to the success
of coastal marine ecosystems, bringing up cold, nutrient-dense water that feeds primary-producing 
phytoplankton, the foundation of coastal marine food chains. Because of this relationship, phytoplankton 
productivity (using chlorophyll a) is a good proxy for affect of ENSO phase on coastal ecosystem health. 
I want to examine how major ENSO events affect zones of coastal upwelling differently in the Atlantic 
and Pacific via chlorophyll a concentration. I want to examine the temporal difference in effect of ENSO event 
on phytoplankton productivity for these two regions to better understand how ENSO phase affects the 
marine food chain differently.
